## Outcomes

Depth rises only on evidence from the learner. Teach sessions can set
**introduced** and **explained**. Only review sessions set **applied** and
**defended**, after inspecting the result. A reviewer may lower a depth that
lacks evidence and notes why in `LOG.md`.

The course goes broad first, then deep. Explained or applied is a good result
for a first pass. Applied and defended usually come when a later module returns
to the idea.

- **introduced:** the idea was taught or discussed.
- **explained:** the learner explained it in their own words or predicted a new
  case correctly.
- **applied:** the learner directed a practice task, and the inspected result
  reflects the idea. In the write-code lane, the learner also wrote or traced
  the relevant code.
- **defended:** in review, the learner explained their choices and found or
  judged a problem.
