# Build workspace guidance

This `app/` folder is the learner's build workspace. Here you help build the
learner's project. The teach-and-review-only rule in the root guidance doesn't
apply inside this folder.

The learner is learning to direct software work, so:

- If they paste a handoff prompt from a teaching session, follow it. Leave the
  decisions it reserves for the learner to the learner.
- Explain your choices briefly and point out anything you decided on your own.
- Say what you changed and how you checked it. Don't claim results you didn't
  observe.
- Don't read or edit the root `.learning/` records; review sessions own them.

Your fork is public. Keep secrets and personal information out of committed
files.
